Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026 · Last updated November 2, 2026

Pack (pack.spruceandsound.com) is a packing-list app run by Spruce & Sound, based in Lynden, Washington ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use Pack, why, who helps us run it, and the choices you have.

The short version
  • We collect what you put into Pack (your email, name, trips, lists, expenses and Venmo username) so the app works.
  • We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • Your item lists are sent to an AI service to sort them. Destinations are sent to a weather service.
  • You can download everything or delete your account any time from the You tab.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address (used to sign you in with a one-time code) and the name you choose to show to people you pack with.
  • Trips and lists: trip names, destinations, dates, notes, bags, items, templates and your inventory of personal items and equipment.
  • Shared trips: who you invite, who is on a trip, and who packed or was assigned what.
  • Expenses: what each expense was for, the amount, who paid, how it was split, and whether shares were paid back.
  • Venmo username (optional): if you add it, people on your shared trips can see it so they can pay you back.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you email us.

Information collected automatically

  • Time zone: read from your device so reminders and daily limits follow your local day.
  • Usage: the days you open Pack and how many smart sorts you use each day. We don't record the pages you view or what you tap.
  • Technical logs: our hosting and database providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the time of a request) for security and troubleshooting.
  • On your device: Pack stores your sign-in session, a copy of your lists for offline use, and preferences such as light or dark mode in your browser's local storage. These are not advertising cookies and are not shared with other sites.

We don't collect your precise location, contacts, photos or payment card details. Pack never handles money; Venmo payments happen in Venmo.

How we use it

  • To run Pack: sign you in, save and sync your lists, share trips with the people you invite, and work offline.
  • To sort your items into categories and bags and suggest things you may have forgotten.
  • To show the forecast or typical weather for your destination and dates.
  • To send emails you've asked for or that the service needs: sign-in codes and day-before trip reminders (you can turn reminders off in the You tab).
  • To keep Pack secure, prevent abuse (for example, daily limits on smart sorts), fix problems, and understand how many people use it.
  • To respond to you and to meet legal obligations.

We don't use your information for advertising and we don't sell or "share" it as those terms are defined in California law. We don't make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects based on automated processing.

Who sees your information

People you pack with. When you join or share a trip, the other members see your name, email address, Venmo username (if you added one), and what you add, pack or pay on that trip. Items on your personal list stay private to you.

Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or us.

If the business changes hands. If Pack is sold or merged, your information may transfer to the new owner, who will remain bound by this policy for the information collected under it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, inventory, templates, trips you own that no one else is on, and your personal lists right away. Database backups that may contain it are overwritten within about 30 days. Server logs are kept for a limited time by our providers, typically 30 days or less. On trips shared with others, items you added to the group list stay so the trip still works for them, but they're no longer linked to you.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: You tab → Download my data gives you a copy of everything in your account.
  • Correct it: edit your name, lists and trips in the app at any time.
  • Delete it: You tab → Delete account.
  • Emails: turn trip reminders off in the You tab. Sign-in codes are only sent when you ask for one.
  • Venmo: remove your Venmo username from the You tab any time.

Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Virginia, Washington, the European Economic Area or the UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to appeal a decision we make about your request. You can use the tools above or email hello@spruceandsound.com. We'll respond within the time the law requires, may need to confirm the request comes from you, and won't treat you differently for using your rights. Because we don't sell or share personal information or use it for targeted advertising, there's nothing to opt out of, and we treat browser signals such as Global Privacy Control as a request not to.

If you're in the EEA or UK, we process your information to provide the service you asked for (contract), for our legitimate interests in keeping Pack secure and working, and where required, with your consent. Your information is stored in the United States. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Pack uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), one-time sign-in codes instead of passwords, and database rules that let only the members of a trip read it. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep access to your email account safe, since that's how you sign in. If we learn of a breach affecting your information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

Children

Pack isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use information we already have, we'll let you know in the app or by email before it takes effect.
